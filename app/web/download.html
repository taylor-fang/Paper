Paper for Mac

The Mac app is a quiet window for writing. It saves each paper as a file in a Paper folder in your home folder, with a backup each day, and works without the internet.

DownloadPaper.zip · about 1 MB · macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel

How to install

  1. Open Paper.zip from your Downloads. You'll get Paper.app.
  2. Drag Paper.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Paper. The first time, macOS says it can't check Paper, because it isn't from the App Store or notarized by Apple yet. Click Done.
  4. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Paper, and click Open Anyway. Enter your password, then click Open.

You only do this once. After that, Paper opens like any other app. Apple explains the steps in Open a Mac app from an unknown developer.

Paper is open source, so you can read every line of it, or build it yourself, from the repo on GitHub.
