Paper App for Mac

Download

Compatible with macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel
About 1 MB

How to install

  1. Open Paper.zip from your Downloads.
  2. Drag Paper.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Paper. The first time, macOS will say it can't check Paper, because it isn't available on the App Store yet. Click Done.
  4. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Paper, and click Open Anyway.

You only need to do this once.

Paper is open source, so you can modify anything from the repo on GitHub.
